DewTask is built on a straightforward principle: your tasks and projects belong to you. This policy explains what happens on your device by default and what happens when you optionally connect your own Google account for synchronization.

00 // The Essentials

LOCAL-FIRST

Stored on your PC

Your workspaces, tasks, and hierarchies live in a local SQLite file on your device.

OFFLINE CAPABLE

No mandatory account

Core task management works completely offline without registering an account.

OPT-IN SYNC

Your Google Drive

Optional sync transfers data directly between your PC and your Google Drive appDataFolder.

01 Information We Handle

We distinguish categorically between your local content and optional remote account details:

Client-side task data: All workspaces, folders, tasks, notes, checklists, subtask trees, and local configurations are stored solely on your machine. This data is never sent to us or any remote server without your deliberate activation of cloud features.

Google account information: If you choose to sign in with Google, DewTask receives the basic account information Google provides for authentication, such as your email address and account identifier. This information is used by the application on your device; DewTask does not operate an account server or transmit it to a DewTask-managed backend.

02 Local Storage and Offline Use

DewTask operates by default as a standalone local Windows desktop application. All database records live in an open SQLite database directly within your user profile directory (%APPDATA%\DewTask\dewtask.db).

You retain sovereign control of this database file. You can back it up, move it, or inspect it at any time. The desktop client contains no background telemetry beacons, silent analytic scripts, or tracking services.

03 Google Sign-In and Synchronisation

Using an account is entirely optional. When you choose to authenticate via Google, we access only the minimal basic profile information (your email address and basic profile identifier) required to authenticate your account and confirm device ownership.

If you enable synchronization, DewTask requests access to the hidden appDataFolder area of your own Google Drive. This area is reserved for application-specific data and is not displayed among your regular Drive files. DewTask does not request access to your Gmail, contacts, or calendar. Sync data is transferred directly between the DewTask application and Google Drive; DewTask does not operate cloud servers or store your workspace contents on its own infrastructure.

04 Data Sharing, Security and Retention

DewTask does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We do not run third-party advertising trackers or behavioral targeting platforms.

Storage and security: When synchronization is enabled, DewTask stores its application data in the hidden appDataFolder area of your own Google Drive. Data is transferred directly between the application and Google using Google's Drive API over encrypted HTTPS connections. DewTask does not operate cloud storage servers, and your workspace contents are not uploaded to or stored on DewTask-managed servers. Google stores this data under your Google account and handles it under its own terms and privacy policies.

Retention and deletion: Your local task data remains in the SQLite database on your device. A synchronized copy remains in your Google Drive appDataFolder until it is deleted through DewTask's cloud-data deletion functionality or the applicable Google account controls. Uninstalling DewTask does not necessarily delete data stored in Google Drive. Removing a local task or uninstalling the application should not be assumed to remove a separate synchronized copy unless the application completes that deletion.

Third-party services: Google provides the authentication and Drive storage used for optional synchronization. DewTask does not receive or retain your workspace contents on its own servers.

05 Your Choices

You can use DewTask offline without connecting a Google account, inspect and back up your local SQLite database, and disconnect your Google account. You can also delete DewTask's application data from your Google Drive. Disconnecting your account or uninstalling DewTask does not necessarily delete the data already stored in Drive; remove the synchronized data separately if you no longer want it retained there.

Google sign-in and Google Drive synchronization are optional. If you enable synchronization, the Application connects to Google services to authenticate your account and store application data in your Google Drive. These connections use Google’s APIs and are subject to the permissions you authorize.

Sandbox Constraint (`appDataFolder`)

Synchronization is designed to use Google Drive’s hidden application data area (appDataFolder) for DewTask-specific data. It is separate from the files and folders normally shown in your Drive. The actual access is limited by the Google OAuth permissions granted to the Application.

When synchronization is enabled, the selected DewTask data is transmitted from your device to Google Drive and may be downloaded to other devices where you use DewTask. This means the data leaves your device and is stored by Google; it is not stored on DewTask-managed servers. DewTask does not operate its own cloud storage service or host your workspace content on its infrastructure. Google’s services are governed by Google’s own terms and privacy policy. Sync availability, data consistency, and retention may depend on Google and your network connection.
